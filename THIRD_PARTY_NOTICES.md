# Third-party materials and provenance

QwenForge contains original engine work alongside evaluation materials originating from an ECNU Summer School public evaluation suite, as acknowledged in the README. This document distinguishes provenance from permission to redistribute.

| Repository area | Reported origin | Licensing / redistribution status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `student_engine.py` and original project documentation | QwenForge author | MIT license in repository |
| `scripts/` (evaluation and validation scripts) | ECNU Summer School public evaluation suite, potentially modified | **Independent licensing / redistribution audit pending** |
| `data/` (public evaluation workloads and summaries) | ECNU Summer School public evaluation suite and generated data | **Per-file provenance / redistribution audit pending** |
| `docs/benchmark_spec.md` | ECNU Summer School evaluation specification | **Independent licensing / redistribution audit pending** |

The top-level MIT LICENSE does **not** establish ownership of third-party materials, and this notice must not be read as a license grant from their original rights holders. Availability in a course or a public source does not itself prove permission for unrestricted redistribution or relicensing.

**Maintainer follow-up needed before asserting the entire repository is MIT-licensed:**

1. Identify the origin and any modifications for each evaluation script, data file, and specification.
2. Locate explicit license terms or obtain redistribution permission from the corresponding rights holders.
3. Record copyright attributions, applicable terms, and any restrictions in this notice or in the affected files.
4. If rights cannot be established, replace or remove affected artifacts and document how users can lawfully obtain them.

QwenForge's handwritten inference engine and its performance claims should be evaluated separately from the reuse rights of the benchmark materials. Questions about attribution or a possible rights issue may be raised with the maintainer.
